Signing In

PromptVault uses your Adobe ID to sign in — there's no separate PromptVault password to create or remember.

Standalone web app​

  1. Open PromptVault in your browser.
  2. Click Sign in with Adobe ID.
  3. You'll be redirected to the standard Adobe login page.
  4. After you log in, you're sent straight back to PromptVault, already signed in.

If you followed a link someone shared with you (for example, a link to a specific prompt), PromptVault remembers where you were headed and takes you there automatically once you're signed in — you won't lose your place.

Inside the Adobe Experience Cloud Shell​

If you're opening PromptVault from inside the Experience Cloud Shell, you're signed in automatically using your existing Experience Cloud session — there's no separate login screen.

Staying signed in​

Your session lasts for several hours. If it expires while you're using the app, PromptVault will automatically send you back to the sign-in screen the next time it needs to talk to the server — just sign in again and you'll be right back where you were.

Signing out closes your session in that browser tab. Use Sign out from the profile menu in the top-right corner to do this manually.

tip

Signing into the web app also automatically signs in the Chrome extension — there's nothing extra to configure. See Chrome Extension.
